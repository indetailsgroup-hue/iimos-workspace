# Practical Guide

A short English fixture. It exists to prove that extraction runs end to end on
a Windows path containing spaces, not to exercise the summarizer.

## Planning

State the goal, the evidence you will accept, and the cost before starting work.
Reject a plan whose success criteria cannot be checked by a command.

## Verification

Re-run the check after the change and read the whole output, including the exit
status. A truncated pass is not a pass.
